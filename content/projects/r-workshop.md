A complete workshop package for statistics lecturers: seven guided three-hour sessions supported by deeper reference workbooks.

- **One anchor dataset** carries through days 2 to 7, so participants clean, explore, infer from, model, examine and publish a single coherent analysis.
- **The capstone starts early** (day 2), leaving day 7 for integration, rendering, peer review and presentation.
- **Everything is provided:** curriculum in Markdown, LaTeX and PDF; daily workbooks; starter, final and solution scripts; a Quarto report template; and an evidence log recording what was done each day.

The days run from getting comfortable with R, through data cleaning, graphics, probability and inference, regression and GLMs, to coding statistical models from their equations and reproducible reporting.
